Posters, and uploads of posters and subtitles.
Video itself is never served by Spexflix Server, but by the media server from signed addresses, which ``playback`` in the xref:../graphql-api-guide.adoc#playback[GraphQL API] returns.
`404 Not Found` when the video does not exist or the caller may not see it.

[[poster]]
== Poster

The uploaded poster, else the frame taken while processing, else the poster of the video's spex from Spexregister, else `404 Not Found`.

operation::video-poster-get[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-headers,http-request,response-headers']

[[poster_upload]]
== Poster upload

Replaces the poster.
The image type is read from the file, not from its name; anything but JPEG, PNG and WebP is
`400 Bad Request`.
The answer is the video, as in GraphQL.

operation::video-poster-post[snippets='security,path-parameters,request-parts,request-headers,http-request,response-fields,http-response']

[[subtitle_upload]]
== Subtitle upload

Adds or replaces the subtitles in a language.
SubRip is converted to WebVTT; anything else is `400 Bad Request`.
Subtitles are kept when the video is processed again.

|===
|Parameter|Description

|`language`|The language code, e.g. `sv` or `en`
|`label`|The name the player shows, e.g. `Svenska`

|===
